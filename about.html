THE INSPIRATION

Why We Canned a Cultural Icon

Every Filipino knows the magic of spotting a giant acrylic tank filled with crushed ice and bright, sweet palamig on a scorching afternoon. For decades, that joy lived exclusively on street corners—served in knotted plastic bags with thin red straws.

Plamig was born in 2024 out of a simple question: Why can’t we take that exact street-side refreshment to the beach, the office, or a road trip without it melting or spilling? After 14 months of recipe testing to keep our gulaman bouncy and our flavors authentic without artificial aftertastes, Plamig hit the shelves.
